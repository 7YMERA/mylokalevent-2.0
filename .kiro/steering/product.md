# Product: MyLokalEvent 2.0

## What it is
A platform for discovering, promoting and running local events in Malaysia. It is a
rebuild of the MyLokalEvent university project (github.com/7YMERA/mylokalevent) into a
professional product that real Malaysians can use and that can earn money.

## Launch niche
Fishing and coastal community events first: fishing competitions, coastal markets,
fresh-catch listings and kolam pancing (fishing pond) spots, starting with Terengganu,
Kelantan and Sabah. Everything is modelled as a general event with a category, so other
kinds of local events can be added without schema changes.

## Users
- **Public visitors:** browse and search events without an account.
- **Members:** save events, follow organisers, get reminders.
- **Organisers:** clubs, co-ops, pond operators and businesses who post and manage
  events. Organisers can be verified (for example with an SSM registration number).
- **Admins:** review events and organisers, handle reports, see revenue.

## How it makes money (current assumption, revisit before launch)
1. A small fee per event listing, paid by the organiser.
2. Paid "featured" placement on the home page and search results.
3. Later: ticketing with a per-ticket fee, and subscriptions for frequent organisers.

## Principles
- Malaysian first: Bahasa Melayu and English, MYR, Malaysian payment methods (FPX,
  cards, e-wallets), Malaysian states and districts.
- Trust and safety: verified organisers, moderation before events go public, reporting.
- Privacy by design: comply with Malaysia's PDPA (consent, access and deletion requests,
  breach notification).
- Shareable: every event page must look good when shared on WhatsApp and be indexable
  by search engines.
- Mobile first: most users will be on phones, often on slower connections.
